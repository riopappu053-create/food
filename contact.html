Frequently Asked Questions

Immediate answers to the most common queries regarding visiting our Miami kitchen.

What are your daily operating hours?

Our flagship kitchen is open Sunday through Thursday from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Friday to Saturday from 10:00 AM to 11:30 PM EST.

Do you accommodate severe food allergies?

Yes, our menu clearly labels vegetarian, gluten-free, and dairy-free options. However, please inform our counter staff or catering desk of any specific allergies prior to ordering.

Is advance booking required for catering trays?

We recommend giving us at least 48 hours notice for large party platters and corporate event orders to guarantee fresh pastry preparation and on-time delivery.

Where can visitors park near Suite 101?

Complimentary 30-minute visitor parking stalls are reserved directly in front of our storefront entrance within the Culinary District plaza.
