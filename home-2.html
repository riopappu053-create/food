Tradition & Passion

Generations of Latin Heritage

Discover the profound culinary roots behind every single recipe we serve at our urban kitchen.

Heirloom Masa Flour

We source non-GMO ancestral corn and unbleached artisan wheat to craft dough that delivers an authentic, melt-in-your-mouth texture with an unforgettable crunch.

Slow-Braised Meats

Our skirt steak, free-range chicken, and smoked pork are simmered for up to six hours with fresh garlic, cumin, sweet onions, and authentic smoked paprika.

Farm-Fresh Produce

Every herb, jalapeño, lime, and avocado is delivered fresh daily from regional sustainable farms to guarantee absolute peak flavor in every bite.

Step-by-Step Mastery

The Artisan Crafting Process

Witness the dedicated craftsmanship required to transform simple ingredients into street food masterpieces.

01

Morning Kneading

Dough is hand-mixed every dawn with real butter and cold water to ensure structural flake perfection.

02

Slow Simmering

Fillings simmer patiently in cast-iron cauldrons until meats melt tender and spices fuse completely.

03

Manual Crimping

Our pastry artisans fold and hand-crimp ('repulgue') every single pocket to lock in savory juices.

04

Golden Fry & Serve

Friction-fried or oven-baked to a glowing amber crunch, served instantly with house chimichurri.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding our operating hours, pickup policies, and catering scheduling.

What are your daily operating hours?

Our kitchen is open Sunday through Thursday from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Friday to Saturday from 10:00 AM to 11:30 PM EST.

Do you accommodate severe food allergies?

Yes, our menu clearly labels vegetarian, gluten-free, and dairy-free options. Please inform our staff of any specific allergies prior to ordering.

Is advance booking required for catering trays?

We recommend giving us at least 48 hours notice for large party platters and corporate event orders to guarantee fresh pastry preparation.
