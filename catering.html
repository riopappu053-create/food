Need Help?

Catering FAQs

Everything you need to know about booking, minimum headcounts, and delivery logistics.

What is the minimum guest count for catering orders?

Our catering packages start at a minimum of 10 guests for corporate boxes and 20 guests for fiesta spreads.

How far in advance must I place my order?

We request at least 48 hours notice for standard catering trays. For large galas (100+ guests), we recommend booking 1 week in advance.

Do you provide plates, napkins, and serving utensils?

Yes! All catering packages include eco-friendly plates, napkins, serving tongs, and individual cutlery sets upon request.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. We offer dedicated vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free empanada varieties clearly labeled in every catering box.
