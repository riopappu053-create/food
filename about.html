Guiding Principles

Our Vision & Mission

The foundational philosophy driving every pastry we fold and every community we serve.

Our Mission

To preserve and celebrate authentic Latin culinary heritage by handcrafting exceptional, premium-quality empanadas and street foods using time-honored recipes, ethically sourced farm-fresh ingredients, and uncompromising dedication to genuine hospitality.

Our Vision

To be the most beloved and trusted Latin street food kitchen nationwide, recognized for culinary excellence, sustainable eco-friendly kitchen practices, and bringing communities together around the warmth of shared artisan food.

Where It All Began

Generations of Secret Family Recipes

Founded by master bakers who migrated from Buenos Aires and Bogotá, Fuego & Masa was born out of a profound love for traditional street markets. Every recipe we prepare honors age-old techniques—from hand-folding delicate pastry borders to slow-braising fillings for over six hours.

Uncompromising Quality

Our 4 Pillars of Excellence

The strict guidelines we follow every single day in our flagship culinary kitchen.

Daily Fresh Dough

Never frozen. Our dough is mixed, rolled, and shaped fresh every morning before sunrise.

Slow Braising

Meats are simmered for hours with authentic Latin herbs, garlic, and aromatic vegetables.

Local Farms

We partner with regional farmers for organic cilantro, jalapeños, and fresh dairy produce.

Zero Preservatives

100% natural ingredients with no artificial enhancers, stabilizers, or chemical additives.

Eco-Friendly Kitchen

Committed to Our Planet & Community

We believe authentic street food should nourish both the soul and the earth. That’s why our packaging is 100% biodegradable, our kitchen oil is recycled into sustainable biofuel, and we donate surplus artisan empanadas daily to local community food banks.

Milestones

Our Journey Through the Years

From a single family recipe book to a beloved culinary landmark.

01

The Street Stall Era (1998)

Started as a weekend market stall serving handmade beef empanadas to passionate neighborhood foodies.

02

Flagship Kitchen Launch (2012)

Opened our permanent flagship location featuring custom stone ovens and expanded Latin street food menus.

03

Catering & Nationwide Kits (2024)

Expanded into full-scale corporate catering and launched national delivery for fresh artisan bake-at-home kits.
